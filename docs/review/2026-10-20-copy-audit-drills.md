## 2. Cross-drill terminology inconsistencies

1. **Accidental glyphs.** D1, D2, D3, D4 and D16 show ASCII `F#` / `Bb` in prompts, buttons, neck labels and the feedback bar (source: `chromaToPc` returns `'C#'`/`'Db'`, `theory/fretboard.ts:57-58`; used raw at `d1-name-the-note/D1View.tsx:83,110`, `d2-find-the-note/D2View.tsx:171,175`, `d2-find-the-note/logic.ts:126-129`, `d3-note-wheel/logic.ts:83-94`, `d4-octave-finder/D4View.tsx` prompt, `d16-play-note/logic.ts:225-232`; feedback bar via `expected` at `d1 logic.ts:60`, `d2 logic.ts:90`, `d3 logic.ts:173`, `d16 logic.ts:133`). Every later drill prettifies to `F♯` / `B♭` (`d7-spell-it/logic.ts:89`, `d8-chord-tone/logic.ts:118`, `d12-progression-builder/logic.ts:251-264`, `d13-capo/logic.ts:171`, `d36-name-the-chord/logic.ts:109`, `d40-key-signature/logic.ts:254`). The first drills a beginner meets are the inconsistent ones.
2. **Degree flats.** `b3`/`b7` ASCII in D6 neck and feedback labels (`d6-interval-tap/logic.ts:244`, used at :298 "the b3 (minor 3rd)") and in D7's formula (`d7-spell-it/ContextPanel.tsx:27` `Bm7 = 1 b3 5 b7`; `logic.ts:366`); `♭3`/`♭7` in D8 (`logic.ts:100`), D9 (`logic.ts:113`), D17 (`logic.ts:123`), D36, D37, D38 (`logic.ts:132`).
3. **Degree wording.** D8/D37/D38 say `the ♭3rd`, `the 5th` (`d8 logic.ts:103-108`, `d38 logic.ts:135-140`); D6 says `the b3 (minor 3rd)` (`d6 logic.ts:298`); D7 strips the flat: `toneWord('b3')` → `the 3rd of Bm7` (`d7 logic.ts:259-271`), so `G is the 7th of Bm7` while the button beside it reads `♭7`.
4. **Interval names.** D5 buttons and neck labels are abbreviations `M3`, `m3`, `P5`, `TT` (`d5-interval-name/D5View.tsx:68,132`); D6 uses words `major 3rd`, `perfect 5th` (`d6 logic.ts:236-238`); D36/D40 use words (`a minor 3rd above A`, `d36 logic.ts:300-327`; `a minor 3rd down`, `d40 text.ts:188`). D5's worked example bridges both (`major 3rd (M3)`), nothing else does.
5. **W W H.** `Steps from the tonic: W W H W W W H.` (`d7 ContextPanel.tsx:32`) and `uses each letter once, in steps W W H W W W H` (`d7 logic.ts:364`) never expand W/H; D17 does: `Whole and half steps: W W H W W W H.` (`d17 logic.ts:473`).
6. **Direction words.** `up the neck` / `toward the nut` (`d6 logic.ts:258-259`, `d9 logic.ts:350`), `Up the neck is toward the body` (`d10 D10View.tsx:136-138`), `higher` / `lower` frets (`d4 meta.ts:46`, `d4 ContextPanel.tsx:106`, `d39 logic.ts:124`), and `2 strings up` meaning toward string 1 (`d4 meta.ts:44`). Only D10 defines its terms.
7. **"Chord tones" vs "Chord formula".** Panels heading the same list: `Chord tones of Am7` (`d8 ContextPanel.tsx:61`, `d37 ContextPanel.tsx:38`), `Chord formula` (`d7 ContextPanel.tsx:24`), `Build Am7` (`d38 ContextPanel.tsx:47`).
8. **Chord naming.** D37 writes triads in words and sevenths as symbols (`C major` / `Cm7`, `d37 logic.ts:248-252`); D9/D10/D36 use symbols throughout (`Cm`, `Am7`); D36 feedback-bar form is `Am7 (A minor seventh)` (`logic.ts:129-131`). D13 says `G shapes`/`Am shapes` for chord shapes.
9. **Correct-answer openers.** `Yes: …` (D8 `logic.ts:300`, D36 `:354-356`, D38 `:608`), `That's …` (D9 `:361`, D10 `:388`, D37 `:421`, DPB `:446`, D4 `:300`), `That's it.` (DPB `:462`), plain statement (D2 `:127`, D16, D17 `You played …`). The bar always adds `✓`.
10. **Mic copy** differs between the two mic drills (`d16-play-note/D16View.tsx:30-36,274-300` vs `d17-play-sequence/D17View.tsx:27-34,276-291`): `FretFree can't hear you: microphone access was blocked…` vs `FretFree can't use the mic. Allow the microphone…`; `This browser can't listen for notes.` vs `This browser can't listen to your guitar. Try Safari on iPad, or Chrome.`; `Starting the microphone…` vs `Starting the mic…`; `…listens through the microphone and records nothing.` vs `…listens through the mic; nothing is recorded or sent.`
11. **Open string.** `fret 0 (open)` in D39's feedback bar (`d39 logic.ts:65`), `string 5, open` (D36 `logic.ts:331`), `Open strings` (D13 CapoNeck.tsx:54), `Open string` a11y (D39View.tsx:254).
12. **Spelling/tone.** British throughout (`neighbours` d3, `Cancelled` d12, `anticlockwise` d40); no `practice/practise` or `colour` slips in learner copy. Tone is clear and never patronising; a few prompts are terse fragments (`Its relative minor?`, `Which major key?`, `Within 3 frets, 4 fingers`). Apostrophes: typographic `’` in d13 `logic.ts:312` and DPB `BoxView.tsx:150-155,211`, straight `'` everywhere else.
13. **Half step / semitone.** Learner copy says `half step` everywhere (good); `semitone` appears only in code and comments.
14. **Fret marker / inlay.** Learner copy says `marker`/`fret marker` consistently (D39); `inlay` only in comments. Good.

## 3. Answer-entry points a learner may find confusing

- **Multi-target rounds end on the first wrong tap** (D4, D9, D37, pentatonic box), and `Done` is never explained except D37's fallback line `Tap them in any order. Done ends the round.` (`D37View.tsx:214`). D4/D9/DPB hints (`D4View.tsx` hint, `D9View.tsx:162`, `BoxView.tsx:160`) say nothing about either.
- **D36 root-then-quality**: quality buttons are disabled until a root is tapped; the only cue is the hint `Name its root, then its quality.` and `Root A: now its quality.` (`D36View.tsx:125-126`). "Quality" is not a beginner word.
- **D7 "Spell"**: `Spell F♯ major` (`logic.ts:379`) does not say what spelling means (enter the 7 notes in order); the slot row and ghost letters carry it.
- **D38**: `Fret 9 is out of reach.` (`D38View.tsx:86`) with no reason; `Check` is enabled after one tap, so a half-built chord can be checked by accident.
- **D17 `Show me`** (`D17View.tsx:305`) reads like a hint but ends the attempt and grades it as a miss (`gaveUp`).
- **D39 find mode** and **D2** are well signposted (`Tap anywhere on that fret, or use the arrow keys and Enter.`; `Tap the fret, or use the arrow keys and Enter.`).
- **D12** drag/tap is well explained; `Undo` / `Check` consistent with D7.

## 4. Top 10 copy changes, ranked by learner impact

1. **Use ♯/♭ in the first five drills and the feedback bar.** Current: `{c}` button text, `This is F#. Tap F# to continue.` (`d1-name-the-note/D1View.tsx:83,110`), `Find G on string 5` with `G#` targets (`d2-find-the-note/D2View.tsx:171-175`, `logic.ts:126-129`), `A whole step above E?` answers `F#` (`d3-note-wheel/logic.ts:83-94`), D4/D16 prompts and `That was F#.` (via `expected`, `d1 logic.ts:60`, `d2 logic.ts:90`, `d3 logic.ts:173`, `d16 logic.ts:133`). Proposed: display every pitch class as `F♯` / `B♭`, as D7–D40 already do.
2. **D5 wrong-answer line.** Current: hint paragraph is empty once answered (`d5-interval-name/D5View.tsx:107-110`); the learner sees only `That was M3.` Proposed (in that slot): `You chose m3 (minor 3rd, 3 half steps). From C up to E is 4 half steps: a major 3rd, M3.` and on a correct answer `Major 3rd: 4 half steps up from C.`
3. **D1 wrong-answer line.** Current: nothing beyond the bar (`d1-name-the-note/D1View.tsx:80-88`). Proposed, under the prompt: `You said G. G is one fret lower, at fret 3. Count up from the open E: E F F♯ G G♯.` (correct: `G♯: fret 4 on string 6.`)
4. **D7 scale rule.** Current: `Steps from the tonic: W W H W W W H.` (`d7-spell-it/ContextPanel.tsx:32`). Proposed: `Steps up from the key note: whole, whole, half, whole, whole, whole, half (W W H W W W H). A whole step is two frets, a half step one.` Same expansion for the worked example `uses each letter once, in steps W W H W W W H:` (`logic.ts:364`) → `uses each letter once, going up in whole and half steps (W W H W W W H):`.
5. **D7 chord formula glyphs.** Current: `Bm7 = 1 b3 5 b7` (`d7-spell-it/ContextPanel.tsx:27`) and `Bm7 is 1 b3 5 b7 from B:` (`logic.ts:366`). 6. **D40 prompts gloss their terms.** Current: `Its relative minor?` and `Which key is its V?` (`d40-key-signature/text.ts:86,88`). 7. **D36 entry hint.** Current: `Name its root, then its quality.` / `Root A: now its quality.` (`d36-name-the-chord/D36View.tsx:125-126`). Proposed: `Tap its root (the lowest note), then its type: major, minor, 7…` / `Root A. Now tap the chord type.`
8. **Multi-target rounds say how they end.** Current: `The square is the root, C, on string 5.` (`d9-build-caged/D9View.tsx:162`), `Start from the A in the square and use octave shapes.` (`d4-octave-finder/D4View.tsx` explain line), `The squares are the roots, A.` (`d-pentatonic-box/BoxView.tsx:160`). Proposed: append ` Tap the notes in any order. 9. **D38 notices and panel.** Current: `Fret 9 is out of reach.` (`d38-build-the-chord/D38View.tsx:86`); `This is one voicing of Am7 from this root.` (`:159`); panel `Within 3 frets, 4 fingers` (`ContextPanel.tsx:66`). 10. **One mic vocabulary.** Current: D16 `This browser can't listen for notes.` / `Starting the microphone…` / `Play on your guitar. ## 5. Overall

The strongest teaching copy is in D7, D11, D12, D13 and D38, which name the misconception (enharmonic letter, wrong quality, rotation, wrong capo direction, missing tone) and give a method. The weakest is the earliest material: D1, D3, D5 and D39 rely entirely on the shared bar `That was X.`, which marks wrong without saying why, which is exactly where the F1 ("no sharp between B–C/E–F") and F2 ("counting from the nut") misconceptions need targeting. Worked examples (rule 8) mostly show the answer rather than a reusable method outside D5, D6, D7, D11, D13 and D40. No design-rule conflicts were found; British spelling and a plain, unpatronising tone are consistent.
